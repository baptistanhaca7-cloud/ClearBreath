The real reason

Why your spray stopped working

Decongestant sprays work by shrinking the blood vessels lining your nose. That's fast relief — for a while. But after about three days of continuous use, those vessels start depending on the medication just to stay open.

When the spray wears off, they swell back up worse than before — so you reach for the bottle again, sooner each time.

Doctors call this rebound congestion, or rhinitis medicamentosa. It's not a lack of willpower. It's a chemical cycle, and the way out isn't a stronger spray — it's a completely different approach.
